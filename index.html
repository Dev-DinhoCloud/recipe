Smoky One-Pot Jollof Rice

A rich, tomato-and-pepper rice with a gentle smoky edge, cooked in a single pot so the grains soak up every drop of flavour.

Recipe by Azeez Abiodun, published 18 October 2026

Jollof is a party staple across West Africa, and everyone has a strong opinion about how it should be made. This version keeps things simple: blend the base, fry it down until it deepens in colour, then let the rice steam gently until tender.

Recipe at a glance

Recipe details
Detail Value
Prep time 20 minutes
Cook time 50 minutes
Serves 6 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

For the pepper base

  • 6 ripe plum tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (tatashe), deseeded
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper, or to taste
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped

For the rice

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice, rinsed
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet, and half the onion until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Fry the remaining sliced onion until golden, then stir in the tomato paste for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the blended base and cook for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring often, until the oil rises to the surface and the sauce darkens.
  4. Stir in the stock, bay leaves, thyme, curry powder, and salt. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Add the rinsed rice and stir once. Cover with foil and a tight lid, then cook on low heat for 25 minutes.
  6. Let the rice rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.

Tip: for the smoky edge, leave the bottom layer of rice to toast for a few extra minutes before serving.

Serving and storage

Serve with fried plantain, grilled chicken, or a simple cabbage salad. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to three days; reheat with a splash of water. For more on rice safety, see the NHS guide to storing and reheating rice.
